# Canonical Roster

## Visual identities

| Member | Canonical appearance |
| --- | --- |
| Central Bank / Fed | Large dark navy ball; black top hat with small Fed badge; gold monocle; optional small dove. |
| Commercial Banks | White ball; two white eyes; gray classical bank icon; red tie. |
| Deposits | White ball; two white eyes; `DEPOSITS`; gray safe icon; red tie. |
| Investment Bank / Primary Dealer | Red and dark ball; black sunglasses; red tie; optional `We advise...` caption. |
| Mutual Funds | Yellow ball; two white eyes; `MF`. |
| Money Market Funds | Smaller yellow-gold ball; two white eyes; `MMF`. |
| GSE / FHLB | Orange ball; two white eyes; three complete orange-roofed white houses on top; optional `FHLB` below eyes. |
| Hedge Fund | Black ball; sharp white eyes; optional separate `LEVERAGE` mug. |
| Supra | White ball; two white eyes; black cloak or coat motif. |

## Money-market participation

Use these mappings as a baseline and verify time-sensitive eligibility before publication.

| Rate or facility | Direct or central actors | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ON RRP | Fed, eligible banks, primary dealers, eligible 2a-7 MMFs, GSEs | Eligible counterparties place cash with the Fed overnight. |
| EFFR / federal funds | Commercial Banks, GSEs especially FHLBs | FHLBs are major lenders but cannot earn IORB; eligible banks borrow. |
| IORB | Fed and eligible depository institutions | MMFs, GSEs, hedge funds, and ordinary mutual funds cannot earn IORB directly. |
| SOFR / repo | Investment Banks, Commercial Banks, MMFs, Hedge Funds; sometimes Mutual Funds and GSEs | Dealers and banks commonly borrow cash; MMFs commonly lend. |
| SRF | Fed, primary dealers, eligible depository institutions | Other roster members cannot borrow directly from the facility. |

## Scale and expression

- Preserve two visible eyes on every scale variant.
- Preserve the smallest identity-bearing icon: bank facade, safe, `MF`, `MMF`, houses, sunglasses, or cloak.
- Use eye shape for emotion: round for neutral, downward for sad, inward-slanted for stern, upward crescents for happy or quietly scheming.
- Never add mouths or limbs to compensate for a small size.
