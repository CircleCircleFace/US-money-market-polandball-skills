# Members of the Finance Polandballs

Treat the roster as an identity lock. Do not redesign a member to fit a panel.

## Required References

Read [references/roster.md](references/roster.md) before selecting or depicting characters.

Use [assets/finance-polandball-lineup.png](assets/finance-polandball-lineup.png) as the authoritative visual reference when it is present and image inputs are supported.

Also apply `$finance-polandball-style` for drawing, dialogue, composition, and no-limb rules.

## Workflow

1. Identify the financial market, facility, or causal mechanism.
2. Select only eligible or narratively necessary members from the roster.
3. Copy each selected member's body color, eyes, labels, and identity accessories.
4. Preserve identity markers when scaling down. Never replace a member with a generic circle, building, or logo.
5. Inspect small characters at final size. They must still have two visible eyes and their defining icon or label.

## Identity Locks

- Draw exactly one pair of white Polandball eyes unless an established accessory covers part of one eye.
- Never let an accessory read as extra eyes, horns, or limbs.
- Keep all characters limbless.
- Keep role labels secondary to faces. Do not place a label across or between the eyes.
- When the same member appears in multiple panels, keep its colors and accessories consistent.

## GSE and FHLB Lock

- Depict FHLB with the canonical GSE body: one orange ball, exactly two normal white eyes on the orange face, and three complete small houses sitting on top.
- Give each house an orange triangular roof and a white square body with a tiny dark doorway. Houses are head accessories, never eyes or floating triangles.
- Put `FHLB` below the eyes only when the story needs the specific institution identified.
- Express sadness, embarrassment, or scheming by reshaping the two eyes; never remove them.

## Small Commercial Bank and Deposits Lock

- A small Commercial Bank remains a white ball with two visible white eyes, gray bank icon, and red tie.
- A small Deposits ball remains a white ball with two visible white eyes, `DEPOSITS`, gray safe icon, and red tie.
- Reduce empty space and icon size before shrinking the eyes. The eyes must remain readable.
- Small borrowers may use happy or scheming crescent eyes, but retain their bank or deposits identity markers.

## Validation

- Confirm every depicted actor exists in the roster or is explicitly introduced as a new exception.
- Confirm each actor is eligible for the depicted facility or clearly shown as excluded.
- Confirm no scaled character lost eyes, labels, or accessories.
- Confirm GSE houses cannot be mistaken for eyes.
